  • When we refer to page count, it includes what you generally think of as a page. Any unique url that promotes or provides a function for your business, not counting those that are part of a paid add-on.

    The homepage, landing pages, product pages, or informational pages (ie, "About Us, Contact Us") are all typical examples of a "page". It could be dedicated to a single topic, or contain multiple sections. We work with you to determine what makes the most sense for your individual business as part of the design process. For example, a dedicated contact page is most often important for businesses that book many of their services over the phone or email, while for others a simple email form could be safely included as part of the homepage or footer without needing it's own url.

    We also don't typically count minor auxillary pages like Privacy Policies or Terms of Service towards your page count, as long as they are of reasonable length and don't require frequent updates.
